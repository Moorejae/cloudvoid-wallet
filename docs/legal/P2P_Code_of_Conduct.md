# CloudVoid P2P Merchant Code of Conduct

## 1. Operational Integrity & Requirements
To maintain "Trusted Merchant" or verified status on the CloudVoid platform, merchants must adhere to the following strict operational requirements:
*   **Minimum Trade Volume:** Must maintain a minimum of 200 successful P2P trades.
*   **Completion Rate:** Must maintain a 100% trade completion rate. Unjustified cancellations are strictly prohibited.
*   **Release Time:** Must maintain an average asset release time of under 5 minutes.
*   **Honest Pricing:** Must honor advertised prices, quantities, and minimum/maximum trade limits.

## 2. Prohibited Activities
Engaging in any of the following activities will result in immediate investigation and potential revocation of merchant status:
*   **Off-Platform Communication:** All trade-related communication must occur within the official CloudVoid P2P chat. Redirecting users to external channels (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram) is strictly forbidden.
*   **Third-Party Payments:** Merchants must use payment accounts that match their verified platform identity. Accepting or sending payments via third-party accounts is a severe violation.
*   **Market Manipulation:** Attempting to manipulate market prices, coordinating trades to induce others, or posting deceptive advertisements.
*   **Fraudulent Chargebacks:** Attempting to profit from chargebacks, refunds, or falsified payment documentation.

## 3. Dispute Resolution & The Jury System
*   Merchants must fully cooperate during arbitration and provide requested evidence promptly.
*   Merchants are required to respect the verdicts delivered by the decentralized CloudVoid Jury System. Continued circumvention or repeated disputes will result in a permanent ban.

## 4. Sanctions & Consequences
CloudVoid defines misconduct broadly to protect the ecosystem. Standard penalties for violating this Code of Conduct include:
*   **Immediate Revocation:** Loss of "Pro Merchant" or "Trusted Merchant" status.
*   **Account Bans & Flagging:** Permanent bans from the CloudVoid platform and flagging of identity to prevent the creation of new accounts.
*   **No Asset Freezing:** CloudVoid does not hold or confiscate merchant assets. The platform relies exclusively on the decentralized Jury to release funds in escrow during a dispute.
*   **Legal Action:** CloudVoid reserves the right to report serious misconduct or fraud to local law enforcement authorities.
